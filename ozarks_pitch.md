## Slide 6: Experience Flow
**Headline:** Experience Flow
**Section 1: Arrival & Check In / Departure & Check-Out**
Guests arrive by luxury watercraft across the Lake of the Ozarks, entering the park through the cavernous Gates of Perdition on the water. After a quick orientation at the Crimson Threshold Guest Center, guests cross the subterranean "River Styx" into the park and up to the lavish, Gothic-style cliffside hotel, Shadowfell. Upon departure, guests receive a leather-bound account of their "Journey through the Underworld" with a beautiful brooch of obsidian wings symbolizing their redemption.

**Section 2: Accommodations**
Ultra-premium Phantom Chamber suites carved into the cave walls with private butlers, massive fireplaces, luxurious furnishings & private infinity pools overlooking the lake. Premium Demonarchy Elite suites inspired by Hell's Royal hierarchy, and decadent lake cabins with private heated docks.
