# Project Pivot: "Hell On Earth" to the Ozarks

While losing the literal name "Hell, Michigan" is a setback, moving to the Lake of the Ozarks offers a massive, perhaps even *better*, creative opportunity. The Ozarks provide a highly thematic geographical canvas that Hell, MI lacked: **Caves, isolation, and deep American folklore.**

Here is the plan for how we should pivot the project and what our first steps will be.

## User Review Required

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The New Branding Angle:** Since we can no longer rely on the literal town name of "Hell," I propose we pivot the brand from a generic "Hell" to **"The Underworld."**

> The Ozarks are famous for having over 300 labyrinthine cave systems and deep, dark lakes. We can frame the resort as an immersive descent into a subterranean realm, leaning into local folklore like Ozark witchcraft and the legendary horned "Ozark Howler" cryptid.

## Proposed Changes

We will tackle the project pivot in three phases.

### Phase 1: Overhaul the Core Narrative & Pitch Deck
We will rewrite the content of your original Keynote pitch to reflect the new location and strategy.
- **Location Update:** Shift from Michigan to Lake of the Ozarks, MO.
- **New USP:** Instead of "Hell sells itself," the new hook will be "The world's first subterranean luxury immersive resort, built into the ancient cave systems of the Ozarks."
- **Experience Flow:** Guests will no longer arrive by a simple road. They will arrive by boat across the Lake of the Ozarks, entering the resort through a massive, illuminated cavern on the water.

### Phase 2: Create the Master Business Plan
Your pitch deck mentions that a "Full Business Plan" and "Detailed Scaling Roadmap" are available upon request. We need to actually build these documents so you are ready when investors ask.
- I will draft a comprehensive 10-15 page Business Plan artifact outlining the financial model, target demographics (luxury Midwestern travelers), and operational logistics.

I will update the "Marketing Maven" HTML vision board we looked at earlier. We will adjust the branding from "Infernal Gastronomy" to things that fit the Ozark landscape, like "Abyssal Cave Dining" and "Dark Water Transport."
